# 🟠 Bitcoin (cbBTC)

## What is the Bitcoin Account?

The Mamo Bitcoin Account gives your Bitcoin a clear, practical use. You deposit cbBTC, which is Bitcoin backed 1:1 by BTC held in Coinbase custody, and Mamo puts your Bitcoin to work where your balance can grow. Growth comes from interest paid by borrowers and from rewards generated by the underlying lending markets. All earnings compound back into more Bitcoin over time, with no manual steps.

{% hint style="info" %}
**About cbBTC.** [cbBTC](https://www.coinbase.com/cbbtc) is Coinbase Bitcoin. Each cbBTC represents Bitcoin held 1:1 in [Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com) custody. cbBTC stays fully redeemable and keeps the same Bitcoin exposure, while allowing your balance to earn inside the [Base](https://www.base.org/) ecosystem.
{% endhint %}

## How your Bitcoin grows

When you deposit cbBTC, Mamo allocates your funds between two trusted destinations on [Base](https://www.base.org/): [Moonwell](https://moonwell.fi/) and [Morpho](https://morpho.org/). Both are lending platforms. Borrowers use them to access Bitcoin liquidity and pay interest in return, and that interest flows back to you.

| **Destination** | **Where your cbBTC goes**                                                                |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Moonwell        | [Moonwell cbBTC Core Market](https://moonwell.fi/markets/supply/base/cbbtc) on Base      |
| Morpho          | [Moonwell cbBTC Frontier Vault](https://moonwell.fi/vaults/deposit/base/mwcbbtc) on Base |

Mamo monitors both destinations continuously and reallocates your cbBTC when rates change. Funds move only when higher returns appear. You do not select markets or adjust positions. Allocation stays automatic and driven by rates.

## What you earn

Your Bitcoin grows in two ways.

* **Borrower interest.** Interest accrues directly to your cbBTC balance for as long as your funds remain deposited. This forms the base layer of growth.
* **Reward tokens.** The underlying market and vault also distribute WELL and MORPHO rewards. Once your rewards reach about $5 in value, Mamo converts them into cbBTC and adds the cbBTC to your balance. Rewards never need manual claiming. Everything feeds back into more Bitcoin.

## How compounding works

Interest and converted rewards are reinvested into your cbBTC balance automatically. Each addition then earns alongside your original deposit, so growth builds on earlier growth without any input from you.

Mamo tracks real-time rates across the market and the vault and reallocates when rates change. This keeps your Bitcoin positioned where earnings are higher, without constant movement or tuning.

## How reward conversions are handled

WELL and MORPHO rewards are converted into cbBTC through [CoW Swap](https://swap.cow.fi/), with prices verified against [Chainlink](https://chain.link/) price feeds. These checks protect each conversion from poor pricing, so rewards convert at fair market value before compounding. Waiting until rewards reach about $5 also avoids small, inefficient swaps.

If market conditions are not right, Mamo waits before converting.

## Security and control

Your Bitcoin stays yours throughout the process. Mamo never takes custody. Deposits move into a personal smart contract vault controlled by you, and only you can withdraw from that vault.

You decide when to add funds, pause deposits or withdraw. There are no lockups and no penalties tied to timing.

## Access anytime

You can request a withdrawal of your cbBTC whenever you want. Mamo applies no lockups, no waiting periods and no penalties, and you can pause or resume deposits based on your needs. Withdrawals do depend on the liquidity available in the underlying markets, which the next section explains.

## Available liquidity

Withdrawals depend on the liquidity available in the underlying yield sources. Your cbBTC is lent to borrowers through Moonwell and Morpho, so the amount that can be withdrawn at any moment is the portion that is not currently borrowed. This is called available liquidity.

When a market is heavily borrowed, available liquidity can run short. A withdrawal for more than the available liquidity will not go through.

#### If your withdrawal does not go through

A failed withdrawal moves nothing. Your cbBTC stays in your account. Come back and try again later. Liquidity returns as borrowers repay their loans and as lenders supply more cbBTC to the market.

#### Checking available liquidity

You can see how much liquidity a market has before you withdraw. On Moonwell, open the [Moonwell cbBTC Core Market](https://moonwell.fi/markets/supply/base/cbbtc) and review the market information, then compare the amount you want to withdraw with the liquidity shown. Your balance may be split between the Moonwell market and the Morpho vault, so treat the market figure as a guide, not an exact limit.

Moonwell's documentation covers [checking available liquidity](https://docs.moonwell.fi/moonwell/moonwell-overview/lend/withdraw#checking-available-liquidity) and [market utilization and liquidity](https://docs.moonwell.fi/moonwell/moonwell-overview/lend/lend-faq#market-utilization-and-liquidity) in more detail.

## Bitcoin from the Mamo Drop

If you also hold MAMO in a [MAMO Account](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/mamo-mamo) with the Reinvest setting, Bitcoin rewards from the weekly Mamo Drop are sent to your Bitcoin Account, where they join your balance.

## Things to know

* **Price risk.** The account grows the amount of Bitcoin you hold. The dollar value of that Bitcoin still rises and falls with the price of Bitcoin.
* **Rates vary.** Interest rates change with borrower demand, so the rate you see today can be different tomorrow.
* **Liquidity.** Withdrawals depend on the liquidity available in the underlying markets. If a market is heavily borrowed, a withdrawal may have to wait until liquidity returns.
* **Smart contract risk.** Independent audits and a bug bounty reduce the chance of vulnerabilities, but no code is perfect.
* **Protocol risk.** Your funds are lent through Moonwell and Morpho on Base. These platforms have strong track records and multiple audits, but they are not backed by government insurance.

[Security and Risk](https://docs.mamo.bot/behind-the-scenes/how-mamo-keeps-you-safe) covers these in more detail.
