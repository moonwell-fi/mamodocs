# 🟣 Ethereum (ETH)

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Your Ethereum Mamo v1.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## What is the Ethereum Account?

The Mamo Ethereum Account gives your Ethereum a clear job. You deposit ETH, and Mamo takes over to grow your balance over time.

Ethereum often sits idle because deciding what to do next feels complex and time consuming. Mamo removes that decision and replaces the guesswork with a steady process designed to run without constant attention. Your ETH stays productive while your focus stays elsewhere.

Control stays with you throughout. You choose when to add funds, when to pause and when to withdraw. There are no lockups, no penalties and no pressure to keep checking or adjusting. The account exists to support long-term holding, with a system built for consistency.

## How your Ethereum grows

After you deposit ETH, Mamo allocates your balance across two established lending destinations on [Base](https://www.base.org/): [Moonwell](https://moonwell.fi/) and [Morpho](https://morpho.org/). Both serve the same purpose. They let borrowers access ETH in exchange for fees, and those fees flow back to depositors as earnings.

| **Destination** | **Where your ETH goes**                                                              |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Moonwell        | [Moonwell ETH Core Market](https://moonwell.fi/markets/supply/base/eth) on Base      |
| Morpho          | [Moonwell ETH Flagship Vault](https://moonwell.fi/vaults/deposit/base/mweth) on Base |

Mamo continuously monitors rates across both destinations and shifts your ETH toward the one paying more. You do not need to choose between them or track their performance. Allocation decisions are made automatically, guided by live market conditions and not by static rules.

## What you earn

Ethereum deposited through Mamo earns in two ways.

* **Borrower interest.** Interest accrues continuously as people pay to use ETH from the underlying market or vault. This interest increases your ETH balance directly over time and forms the base layer of growth.
* **Reward tokens.** The underlying markets also distribute extra incentives to depositors, in the form of WELL and MORPHO reward tokens. Once your rewards reach about $5 in value, Mamo converts them into ETH and adds the ETH to your balance.

There is no claiming, no swapping and no secondary asset left behind. Everything goes back into more Ethereum.

## How compounding works

Interest and converted rewards rejoin your balance automatically, so compounding continues without interruption. Each addition earns alongside your original deposit, and growth builds on earlier growth.

Mamo tracks real-time rates across the supported market and vault and reallocates your ETH when meaningful differences appear. This avoids guesswork and reduces the opportunities missed by leaving funds in one place.

## How reward conversions are handled

WELL and MORPHO rewards are converted into ETH through [CoW Swap](https://swap.cow.fi/), with prices verified against [Chainlink](https://chain.link/) price feeds. These checks are designed to keep each conversion in line with fair market value. Waiting until rewards reach about $5 also avoids small, inefficient swaps.

If market conditions are not right, Mamo waits before converting.

## Security and ownership

Ethereum deposited through Mamo always remains under your control. Mamo never takes custody. Your funds move into a personal smart contract vault tied to your wallet, which leaves authority in your hands from start to finish. Deposits, pauses and withdrawals follow your direction alone.

## Access anytime

You can request a withdrawal of your ETH whenever you want. Mamo applies no lockups and no penalties, and you can pause or resume deposits on your own schedule. Withdrawals do depend on the liquidity available in the underlying markets, which the next section explains.

## Available liquidity

Withdrawals depend on the liquidity available in the underlying yield sources. Your ETH is lent to borrowers through Moonwell and Morpho, so the amount that can be withdrawn at any moment is the portion that is not currently borrowed. This is called available liquidity.

When a market is heavily borrowed, available liquidity can run short. A withdrawal for more than the available liquidity will not go through.

#### If your withdrawal does not go through

A failed withdrawal moves nothing. Your ETH stays in your account. Come back and try again later. Liquidity returns as borrowers repay their loans and as lenders supply more ETH to the market.

#### Checking available liquidity

You can see how much liquidity a market has before you withdraw. On Moonwell, open the [Moonwell ETH Core Market](https://moonwell.fi/markets/supply/base/eth) and review the market information, then compare the amount you want to withdraw with the liquidity shown. Your balance may be split between the Moonwell market and the Morpho vault, so treat the market figure as a guide, not an exact limit.

Moonwell's documentation covers [checking available liquidity](https://docs.moonwell.fi/moonwell/moonwell-overview/lend/withdraw#checking-available-liquidity) and [market utilization and liquidity](https://docs.moonwell.fi/moonwell/moonwell-overview/lend/lend-faq#market-utilization-and-liquidity) in more detail.

## Ethereum from the Mamo Drop

If you also hold MAMO in a [MAMO Account](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/mamo-mamo) with the Reinvest setting, Ethereum rewards from the weekly Mamo Drop are sent to your Ethereum Account, where they join your balance.

## Things to know

* **Price risk.** The account grows the amount of ETH you hold. The dollar value of that ETH still rises and falls with the price of Ethereum.
* **Rates vary.** Interest rates change with borrower demand, so the rate you see today can be different tomorrow.
* **Liquidity.** Withdrawals depend on the liquidity available in the underlying markets. If a market is heavily borrowed, a withdrawal may have to wait until liquidity returns.
* **Smart contract risk.** Independent audits and a bug bounty reduce the chance of vulnerabilities, but no code is perfect.
* **Protocol risk.** Your funds are lent through Moonwell and Morpho on Base. These platforms have strong track records and multiple audits, but they are not backed by government insurance.

[Security and Risk](https://docs.mamo.bot/behind-the-scenes/how-mamo-keeps-you-safe) covers these in more detail.
