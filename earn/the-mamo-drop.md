# 💧 The Mamo Drop

The Mamo Drop is the weekly payout that shares Mamo's fees with MAMO depositors. The drop lands every Wednesday at 0:00 UTC. Each week, Mamo gathers the fees collected and splits them among everyone with MAMO deposited in a [MAMO Account](mamo-account.md). A drop can include MAMO, Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDC and any of the 12 stocks available through Mamo.

You do not need to buy a stock or set up a [portfolio](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/stocks) to earn stocks. Deposit MAMO, and stocks can start arriving as part of your weekly rewards.

### How the Mamo Drop works

The Mamo Drop is how Mamo shares what Mamo earns with the people who hold MAMO. As Mamo collects fees, depositors receive a share of them. Each drop follows the same weekly cycle:

1. **Mamo collects fees.** Through the week, Mamo earns trading fees on Aero and collects management fees on stock portfolios.
2. **Mamo announces the drop.** Each week, Mamo posts the drop on X at [@mamo](https://x.com/mamo), with a breakdown of what will be distributed.
3. **Rewards reach depositors.** The drop lands every Wednesday at 0:00 UTC, and the fees are split among depositors. Rewards then stream to your account continuously through the week, not in a single lump sum.
4. **Your rewards are handled.** Rewards show up in your MAMO Account, where you can see what you have earned. Once they are worth more than $1, Mamo compounds or reinvests them according to your reward setting. You can also claim them to your wallet at any time.

#### How your share is worked out

Your share of each drop depends on how much MAMO you have deposited. The more MAMO in your account, the bigger your slice. Depositing more MAMO increases your share of future drops, and withdrawing MAMO reduces your share.

#### Why drops change from week to week

No two drops are the same. The assets and amounts depend on the fees Mamo collected that week. Trading fees rise and fall with trading activity in Mamo's pools. Stock rewards depend on which stocks were traded while portfolios were rebalanced, so one week might include Apple and the next might include Nvidia.

### Where rewards come from

The drop has two sources of rewards. The first is trading fees, which have funded the drop from the start. The second is the management fees Mamo collects on stock portfolios, which is how stocks reach your drop. Both feed the same weekly payout.

#### Trading fees

Mamo earns trading fees from pools on Aero, a decentralized exchange on Base. Every trade in one of these pools pays a small fee. Each week, Mamo shares 100% of the trading fees earned there with MAMO depositors.

The pools pair MAMO with other assets, including:

* MAMO/cbBTC
* MAMO/VIRTUAL
* MAMO/USDC
* MAMO/ETH

Each new pair adds another source of trading fees for the drop.

#### Stock portfolio management fees

A Mamo stock portfolio is a set of stocks that Mamo manages to a mix the owner chooses. Mamo charges a [**1% annual management fee**](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/stocks#fees-and-limits) on these portfolios, and shares those fees with MAMO depositors through the weekly drop.

The fee is paid by people who hold stock portfolios, not by MAMO depositors. If you only hold MAMO, you receive a share of these fees without paying them.

### How stock fees are collected

To see how a stock ends up in your drop, it helps to know how a stock portfolio works.

1. **A portfolio sets target percentages.** The owner chooses which stocks to hold and how much of the portfolio each one should make up. Say Apple is set to 20%.
2. **Prices move the mix.** Stock prices change at different rates. If Apple rises faster than the other stocks, Apple grows beyond 20% of the portfolio. If Apple falls behind, Apple shrinks below 20%.
3. **Mamo rebalances.** When the mix moves far enough from the chosen percentages, Mamo buys or sells stocks to bring the portfolio back toward them. This is called [rebalancing](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/stocks#id-3.-automated-rebalancing). The owner does not need to watch prices or place trades.
4. **The fee is collected during those trades.** Instead of taking the full 1% at once, Mamo collects the fee a little at a time through the year, as part of rebalancing trades.
5. **The fee is collected in the stock being traded.** A fee collected in Apple means Apple can be part of your next drop. A fee collected in Nvidia means Nvidia can be too.

This is why stocks can land in your MAMO Account even if you have never bought one. The stocks in a drop reflect the rebalancing trades Mamo made, which is also why the mix of stocks changes from week to week.

### What happens to your rewards

Your reward setting decides what happens to each drop: Compound or Reinvest. You can also claim to your wallet at any time. Both settings and claiming are covered in full in MAMO Account.

With Compound, everything becomes MAMO, and your next drop is based on that larger balance. With Reinvest, the same drop builds three separate holdings: MAMO, Bitcoin and Apple. With a claim, all three assets sit in your wallet for you to manage yourself.

### FAQ

#### Do I need a stock portfolio to earn stocks?

No. Deposit MAMO into your account. When you earn stocks in your drop, Mamo creates a portfolio for you. To open one yourself, [start with 100 USDC](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/stocks#fees-and-limits).

#### Do I need to buy stocks myself?

No. Stock rewards come from the fees Mamo collects on stock portfolios, not from anything you buy.

#### Do I pay the 1% management fee?

Not as a MAMO depositor. The fee is charged on stock portfolios, and MAMO depositors receive a share of the fees collected. If you also hold a stock portfolio, the fee applies to that portfolio.

#### When do rewards arrive?

Every week. The drop lands on Wednesday at 0:00 UTC. Mamo announces each drop on X, and rewards stream to your account through the week.

#### When are my rewards compounded or reinvested?

Automatically, once your rewards are worth more than $1. Until then, your rewards stay in your MAMO Account, and you can claim them to your wallet at any time.

#### Will I get the same rewards every week?

Not necessarily. The assets and amounts in your drop depend on the fees Mamo collects that week.

#### Why did my drop include a stock I have never bought?

The management fee on stock portfolios is collected in the stock being traded. When Mamo collects a fee in Apple, Apple can be part of the next drop, whether or not you have ever held Apple.

#### Can I change my reward setting?

Yes. Compound is on by default, and you can switch to Reinvest in your MAMO Account.

#### Can I hold stock rewards in my own wallet?

Yes. Claimed rewards arrive in the assets you earned them in, stocks included.

### Availability and risks

* **Availability.** [Stock portfolios](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/stocks) are available in eligible jurisdictions outside the U.S.
* **Stock prices.** Stock values can fall, and you may lose money.
* **Stocks on Base.** Stocks on Base carry [issuer, custody, liquidity and smart-contract risks](https://docs.mamo.bot/behind-the-scenes/how-mamo-keeps-you-safe).
* **Variable rewards.** The assets and amounts in each drop change with the fees Mamo collects. No amount is guaranteed.
* **Not advice.** Nothing here is financial advice.
