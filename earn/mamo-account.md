# 🟢 Mamo (MAMO)

The MAMO Account is where MAMO holders share in what Mamo earns. You deposit MAMO once, and each week a share of Mamo's fees comes back to you as rewards. This page covers how the account works, the two reward settings, and how to claim, deposit and withdraw.

### What is the MAMO Account?

The MAMO Account holds your MAMO and earns rewards on your deposit. While your MAMO is deposited, you receive a share of the fees Mamo collects, paid out every Wednesday at 0:00 UTC through [The Mamo Drop](the-mamo-drop.md).

Your share depends on how much MAMO you have deposited. The more MAMO in your account, the bigger your slice of each drop. There is nothing to manage after the deposit. Mamo works out your rewards for you, and they show up in your account, where you can see what you have earned.

The account stays under your control. Each Mamo account is a smart contract owned by your wallet, so only you can withdraw from yours. You can deposit more or withdraw at any time, and there are no lock-up periods.

### What you earn

Rewards come from two sources of fees: the trading fees Mamo earns on [Aero](https://aero.xyz/), and the management fees Mamo collects on [stock portfolios](../invest/stocks.md). The fees arrive in different assets, so a drop can include MAMO, Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDC and stocks such as Apple and Nvidia.

The amounts are not fixed. What you receive, and how much, changes from week to week with the fees Mamo collects. [The Mamo Drop](the-mamo-drop.md) explains where each reward comes from and which stocks you can earn.

### Benefits

* **A share of Mamo's fees, every week.** Rewards arrive through the weekly drop for as long as your MAMO is deposited.
* **Rewards in more than one asset.** A drop can include MAMO, Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDC and stocks.
* **Automatic by default.** Compound is switched on from the start, so your rewards are put to work without any action from you.
* **Set up once.** Deposit MAMO and let Mamo handle the rest. No daily management is required.
* **Your choice of what happens to rewards.** Compound them into more MAMO, reinvest them across your other accounts, or claim them to your wallet.
* **No lock-ups.** Deposit or withdraw at any time.

### Reward settings

Every MAMO Account has a reward setting that tells Mamo what to do with your rewards. There are two: Compound and Reinvest. Compound is the default, and you can switch between them in your MAMO Account.

Both settings work automatically. Once your rewards are worth more than $1, Mamo handles them according to your setting. Rewards below $1 stay in your MAMO Account until they pass that mark, and you can still claim them to your wallet in the meantime.

#### Compound

Compound turns every reward into MAMO. Each week, your drop might include a mix of MAMO, Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDC and stocks. Once those rewards are worth more than $1, Mamo converts all of them into MAMO and adds the MAMO to your account.

This matters because your share of each drop depends on how much MAMO you have deposited. Every time rewards are added as MAMO, your balance grows, and your share of the next drop grows with your balance. Over time, each drop helps make the next one bigger. That is what compounding means.

Compound suits you if you want to build up your MAMO balance and leave that balance to grow, without managing anything yourself. Keep in mind that every reward ends up as MAMO, so the value of what you earn moves with the price of MAMO.

#### Reinvest

Reinvest keeps your rewards in the assets you earned. Once your rewards are worth more than $1, Mamo sends each one to the account where that asset belongs, instead of converting everything into MAMO. Each reward joins the balance you already hold there, as the table above shows.

Stock rewards need a little more explanation, because a stock portfolio follows a mix of stocks that you chose:

* **You already own the stock.** The reward adds to that holding.
* **The stock is not in the mix you chose.** The stock may show up in your portfolio for a while. Mamo then [rebalances your portfolio](../invest/stocks.md#id-3.-automated-rebalancing) back toward the percentages you set, so the mix you chose is kept.
* **You have no portfolio yet.** Mamo creates one for you when your first stock reward arrives. You can also [open a portfolio yourself](../invest/stocks.md#id-2.-deposit-usdc) with 100 USDC.

Reinvest suits you if you want to spread your rewards across different assets and build up your Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDC and stock holdings over time. Your MAMO rewards still stay in your MAMO Account, so your share of future drops keeps growing too, though more slowly than under Compound.

### Claiming to your wallet

Claiming is the manual alternative to both settings. Instead of letting Mamo compound or reinvest your rewards, you can claim them to your wallet at any time, whichever setting you are on.

Claimed rewards arrive in the assets you earned them in. MAMO arrives as MAMO, Bitcoin as Bitcoin and stocks as stocks, so you can hold stocks in your own wallet. From there, what you do with them is up to you: keep them, turn them into more MAMO, or add them to a stock portfolio.

A claim is a transaction on Base, so your wallet needs a small amount of ETH to cover the network fee.

If you already have an account and see an upgrade prompt in the app, follow the steps there before claiming.

### Deposits and withdrawals

You can deposit MAMO at any time. A deposit starts earning a share of future drops, and adding more MAMO increases your share.

You can also withdraw at any time. There are no lock-up periods. MAMO you withdraw stops counting toward future drops, so your share follows the balance you leave in the account.

### Get started

1. **Open your MAMO Account.** Go to [mamo.bot](https://mamo.bot) and open your MAMO Account.
2. **Deposit MAMO.** Your deposit starts earning a share of future drops.
3. **Check your reward setting.** Compound is on by default. Switch to Reinvest if you would rather keep rewards in the assets you earned.
4. **Watch your rewards arrive.** When rewards come in, you will see what you have earned. Mamo compounds or reinvests them automatically above $1, or you can claim them to your wallet.

### Things to know

* **Rewards vary.** The assets and amounts in each drop depend on the fees Mamo collects that week. No amount is guaranteed.
* **Prices move.** MAMO, Bitcoin, Ethereum and stocks can all fall in value, and you may lose money.
* **Stocks have their own risks and limits.** Stocks on Base carry issuer, custody, liquidity and smart-contract risks. Stock portfolios are available in eligible jurisdictions outside the U.S.
* **This is not financial advice.** For more on how Mamo approaches safety, see [Security and Risk](https://docs.mamo.bot/behind-the-scenes/how-mamo-keeps-you-safe).
