<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Twitter post - 6.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Security at Mamo works quietly in the background, like the rest of Mamo's systems. You already have enough to manage without worrying about whether your money is protected. That is why safety is built into every layer, then verified by independent experts to confirm everything works as intended.

We do not ask for blind trust. We earn trust through transparency, verification and care.

## Why audits matter

Your money deserves more than promises.

An audit is an independent review of the code that holds and moves your funds. Security experts examine the contracts, test how they behave under a wide range of conditions, and report anything that needs fixing. Independent experts have examined Mamo from the ground up in this way, stress-testing the systems, reviewing the code and confirming that everything behaves as intended.

### Sherlock bug bounty

Even after expert reviews, Mamo maintains an active bug bounty program with [Sherlock](https://audits.sherlock.xyz/bug-bounties/350), offering up to $250,000 for responsibly disclosed security issues. The program invites hundreds of independent researchers to search for anything previous reviews might have missed.

**What this means for you:** you benefit from several layers of protection working together to keep your funds secure.

## Ongoing security commitment

Security is not a one-time task. Security is an ongoing practice of improvement and verification. As Mamo evolves, we remain committed to:

* **Regular audits.** Every new feature is independently reviewed before going live.
* **Clear updates.** You will always know what is changing and why.
* **Plain language.** Security is explained in terms that make sense.

Audits lower risk, but no review can remove risk entirely. [Security and Risk](https://docs.mamo.bot/behind-the-scenes/how-mamo-keeps-you-safe) explains the protections around your funds and the risks that remain.
