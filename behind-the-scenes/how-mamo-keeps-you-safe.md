# 🛡️ Security and Risk

Mamo is simple on the surface and secure at the core. Security is designed so that protection never gets in the way of calm, steady growth. While you practise good financial habits, such as regular deposits and long-term thinking, Mamo's systems keep you in control from start to finish.

This page explains how your funds are protected, how rewards are converted safely, and which risks remain.

### 🛡️ Self-custody, no exceptions

When you deposit into Mamo, your funds do not go into a communal pool or an opaque account. They flow directly into a personal smart contract vault that belongs to your wallet alone, much like a safe deposit box where only your key works.

Mamo can guide your funds but never take them. Every action that moves your money, whether a deposit, a withdrawal or a strategy change, requires your signature.

Your money remains accessible at all times. If Mamo is ever offline, you can still reach your funds directly through any standard wallet or block explorer. There is no scenario in which Mamo holds your funds. We build the rails, and you steer the train.

### 🔐 Security by design

Security is built into every part of Mamo's systems, not added as an afterthought.

#### Independent audits

Trust comes from proof, not promises. Mamo has been independently audited by leading security firms, including [Certora](https://www.certora.com) and [Halborn Security](https://halborn.com). The reports are listed on the [Audits](https://docs.mamo.bot/behind-the-scenes/audits) page.

#### $250K bug bounty

Mamo's [Sherlock](https://audits.sherlock.xyz/bug-bounties/350) bug bounty program pays up to $250,000 to white-hat researchers who responsibly disclose vulnerabilities before bad actors can exploit them. A standing reward means continuous scrutiny, and stronger safety over time.

#### Permissioned upgrades

There are no silent contract migrations. Any upgrade to your account requires your explicit approval. If you choose not to approve, nothing changes. No hidden moves, no surprises.

#### Fail-safe access

Your vault is a transparent smart contract on the Base network. Even if Mamo were to go offline, you would retain 100% control of your balance using any Ethereum-compatible wallet.

### 👀 Transparency

Financial stress often comes from hidden rules and unclear terms. Mamo keeps things clear:

* No hidden fees in fine print
* No lockups preventing withdrawals
* No unexpected changes disrupting your progress

Where a fee applies, the fee is stated on the page for that product. Stock portfolios, for example, carry a 1% annual management fee, which the [Stocks](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/stocks#fees-and-limits) page sets out alongside every limit.

### 🧠 Smart protection for your earnings

Earning is valuable, but keeping what you earn safe matters more. When Mamo converts reward tokens such as WELL or MORPHO back into USDC, cbBTC or ETH, three layers of protection apply:

1. [**CoW Swap**](https://swap.cow.fi/) **batch auctions** prevent front-running and "sandwich" attacks.
2. [**Chainlink**](https://chain.link/) **oracles** act as independent price feeds, and halt any swap when market prices deviate from fair value.
3. **Tight slippage limits** make sure you do not lose value during volatile price swings. If conditions are not right, Mamo waits before converting your rewards.

Stock portfolios have their own trading safeguards, including price limits and allocation limits on every rebalance. The [Stocks](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/stocks) page describes them.

### ⚖️ Honest about risk

All investing carries risk. Mamo is upfront about what those risks are.

* **Variable rates.** Interest rates change with borrower demand. A 12% rate today may be 8% tomorrow, or even within the hour. Mamo always seeks the best available rate, but the number will change.
* **Smart contract risk.** Independent audits and bug bounties reduce the chance of vulnerabilities, but no code is perfect.
* **Protocol risk.** Mamo routes funds to Moonwell and Morpho on Base. These platforms have strong track records and multiple audits, but they are not backed by government insurance.
* **Price risk.** Bitcoin, Ethereum, MAMO and stocks can all fall in value. Earning on an asset does not protect you from a fall in that asset's price.
* **Stock-specific risks.** Tokenized stocks carry issuer, custody, liquidity and pricing risks, and are available only in eligible jurisdictions outside the United States. The [Stocks](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/stocks) page sets these out in full.

Our role is to minimise risk, explain risk clearly, and make sure you can adjust your position at any time.
