# 🏗️ Contributors

## The people behind Mamo

Mamo is built by a team who believe wealth-building should not require becoming a financial expert.

We are engineers, designers and problem-solvers with experience at companies such as Coinbase, Apple, Amazon, Twitter/X and Lyft. Our goal is to make wealth-building accessible to everyone, not only those already fluent in the language of finance.

We design with care, test thoroughly and improve continuously, because calm, steady progress is what creates lasting growth. That approach shows in how Mamo is built: every new feature is independently reviewed before going live, a bug bounty stays open to outside researchers, and upgrades to your account need your approval. [Security and Risk](https://docs.mamo.bot/behind-the-scenes/how-mamo-keeps-you-safe) and [Audits](https://docs.mamo.bot/behind-the-scenes/audits) cover the detail.

Mamo is supported by a wider community of builders, designers, moderators and advisors who actively shape Mamo's future. Their ideas, feedback and contributions help Mamo keep growing in ways that serve everyone.
