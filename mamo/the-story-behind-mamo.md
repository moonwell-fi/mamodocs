# 📖 The Story Behind Mamo

Mamo began with a simple question: **why does growing your money feel so complicated, when the process should not have to be?**

In a world full of financial apps that promise everything and often deliver confusion, we wanted something different. Not another tool that forces you to become a financial expert just to take part, but something quieter. Something that works in the background while you live your life, because you already have enough on your plate.

So we built Mamo: a personal finance companion designed for steady growth and quiet confidence, and a system that makes wealth-building feel achievable for everyone, not only for those who speak the language of finance.

### **Built from experience, backed by intention**

Mamo's journey began in 2020, when the world paused and daily routines changed.

During that time, founder Luke Youngblood began exploring smarter ways to grow his money. Not by chasing trends or staring at screens all day, but through calm, consistent systems that compound over time.

As he discovered what worked, friends noticed. Some suggested he start a fund. Luke had a different vision.

He did not want to manage other people's money. He wanted to create a companion that helped people grow their money themselves, without spreadsheets, without confusing crypto jargon and without gatekeepers. Building wealth should not require insider knowledge. Building wealth should be open to anyone with a phone and the desire to begin.

### From idea to action

That vision took shape in 2025 at the [Coinbase Developer AI Hackathon](https://x.com/CoinbaseDev/status/1886468701689631006). Luke teamed up with engineers from leading technology companies to create what would become Mamo: an AI companion that quietly grows your money while explaining everything in human terms.

The prototype won first place.

More importantly, the idea resonated with people. They were not looking for more complexity. They wanted something that simply works in the background, a system built for the many rather than the few.

### What Mamo has become

From that prototype, Mamo has grown into a set of accounts that each do one job well:

* [USDC](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/usd-coin-usdc), [Bitcoin](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/bitcoin-cbbtc) and [Ethereum](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/ethereum-eth) accounts that earn lending interest and compound automatically
* [Stock portfolios](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/stocks) that Mamo rebalances to a mix you choose
* The [MAMO Account](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/mamo-mamo), which shares the fees Mamo collects with MAMO depositors every week through the Mamo Drop

The principles have not changed along the way: plain language, your funds under your control, and steady progress over hype.

### Where we are going

Mamo is only getting started. We are building a companion that helps anyone grow wealth through smart, human-first routines, while you remain in control at all times. Steady wins, and one habit at a time, can lay the foundation for compounding growth for everyone.
