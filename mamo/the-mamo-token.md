# 🌱 The MAMO Token

MAMO is the token at the centre of Mamo. This page explains what the token is for, where to find the token on Base, and how the supply was distributed.

## What MAMO is for

Holding MAMO lets you share in what Mamo earns. When you deposit MAMO into a [MAMO Account](https://docs.mamo.bot/grow/mamo-mamo), you receive a share of the fees Mamo collects, paid out every week through the Mamo Drop. Your share depends on how much MAMO you have deposited.

## Token distribution

From the beginning, distribution has followed the same principles that guide everything we do: transparency, thoughtfulness and alignment with the community.

There are no venture capital allocations. There are no day-one unlocks for the team, the treasury or platform development. There are no hidden mechanisms. The distribution is designed to be fair, transparent and aligned with the people who matter most: you.

#### Community airdrop (10%)

Mamo exists because of the communities that helped shape the project. 10% of the total supply is reserved for people in the following communities:

* stkWELL holders
* WELL holders
* VIRTUAL stakers
* veAERO holders
* MEME holders

This is our way of saying thank you, and an invitation to grow with us from day one. If you are one of these holders or stakers, you can claim your MAMO airdrop [here](https://app.sablier.com/airdrops/campaign/0x72d76fb5fc4bf4b0fc905561fef7f36b4a71e4e7-8453/).

#### Treasury (25%)

The treasury supports the long-term growth of the Mamo ecosystem through grants, community initiatives and strategic partnerships. These tokens vest monthly over 24 months, with nothing unlocked at launch.

#### Platform development (25%)

This allocation is dedicated to improving Mamo through new features, integrations and infrastructure enhancements. These tokens also vest monthly over 24 months, with nothing unlocked at launch.

#### Team (15%)

This allocation goes to the contributors who built Mamo and continue to make Mamo smarter, more secure and more helpful every day. Team tokens have a 6-month cliff, then vest monthly over 24 months. Nothing was unlocked at launch.

#### Virtuals LP (15%)

These tokens are locked. They provide liquidity in the MAMO/VIRTUAL pool, which supports efficient trading with minimal slippage.

#### cbBTC LP (10%)

These tokens are locked. They provide liquidity in the MAMO/cbBTC pool on [Aero](https://aero.xyz/). cbBTC is Coinbase's version of Bitcoin, and the pairing connects Mamo more closely with the wider ecosystem.

## Why liquidity matters

A liquidity pool holds two tokens so that people can trade between them. The deeper the pool, the less each trade moves the price. Trading in Mamo's pools also produces trading fees, and those fees are shared with MAMO depositors through the weekly Mamo Drop.
